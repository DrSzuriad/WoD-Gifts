### Dokko

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 5
Gnoza: 4
Strona PDF: 94–95
Strona drukowana: 93–94

Pełny opis:

A Dokko is a sorcerer’s dagger. This dagger has three short blades on both ends of the handle. On a successful melee attack, along with the expenditure of a Gnosis point, it bites into the flesh of the opponent and will remain imbedded. The sorcerer can cast spells at the victim through the dagger, no matter how far the target may run. If the dagger is pulled out of the victim’s body, it causes two health levels of damage, but the “homing effect” is negated. If the owner ever loses the dagger, he will find it among his possessions after three days.
