## FETYSZE

### Firebird Feather

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: nie podano
Gnoza: nie podano
Strona PDF: 51
Strona drukowana: 56

Pełny opis:

There are two ways of gaining the attention of the Firebirds. The first is to build a large collection of knowledge. The other is by acquiring one of their feathers as a fetish. Firebird feathers may create a direct mental link to the bird it came from (costing 2 Gnosis to activate). The Firebird may decide if it wishes to answer such an invitation. Firebirds may make gifts of their feathers to scholars whom they find particularly promising.
