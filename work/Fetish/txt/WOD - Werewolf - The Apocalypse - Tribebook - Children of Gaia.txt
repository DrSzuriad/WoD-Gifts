## FETYSZE

### Truth Feather

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 2
Gnoza: 6
Strona PDF: 54
Strona drukowana: 53

Pełny opis:

This fetish is a single falcon feather. It provides the level one Philodox Gift Truth of Gaia.

### Goggles of Sight From Beyond

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 3
Gnoza: 6
Strona PDF: 54

This fetish can be a pair of sunglasses, but is usually a pair of heavy-duty goggles. The goggles provide the level two Theurge Gift, Sight from Beyond.

---

### Gaia's Ghost Shirt

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 5
Gnoza: 7
Strona PDF: 54
Strona drukowana: 53

Pełny opis:

This fetish is a vest made of reeds. Gaia deflects wounds from the wearer.

The shirt adds two extra soak dice. If the Child of Gaia does not strike his foe, but only tries to subdue him, the shirt adds four soak dice. If the Child of Gaia refuses to fight back at all, the shirt adds five soak dice. The shirt also negates damage from the level five Get of Fenris Gift Fenris' Bite, the level four Shadow Lords Gift Open Wounds, and the Black Spiral Dancer Gifts of Foaming Fury, Crawling Poison, and Balefire.

---

### Memory Ribbon

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: nie podano
Gnoza: nie podano

Pełny opis:

You also have a fetish, a Memory Ribbon from the Atrocity Realm in the Umbra. It serves as a reminder of the atrocities you fight to prevent (you may not be possessed and all frenzy difficulties are raised by two).
