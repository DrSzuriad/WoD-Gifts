### Mandala

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 3
Gnoza: 7
Strona PDF: 56
Strona drukowana: 54

Pełny opis:

Many Stargazers devise their own mandalas, personal portraits of their inner symbology designed in a symmetrical circle or square. Such an image is a powerful meditational tool. Each Stargazer must make his own; using another's mandala provides no benefit.

Once activated, a mandala will restore one point of lost Willpower and allow a meditation roll to restore Gnosis with a difficulty of only 7. Such powers can be used once per day.
