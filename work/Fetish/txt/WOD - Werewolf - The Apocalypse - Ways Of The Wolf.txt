## FETYSZE

### Toolstick

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 3
Gnoza: 6
Strona PDF: 53
Strona drukowana: 53

Pełny opis:

This rather simple stick, a branch broken off of a tree, can transform into any tool: hammer, wrench, etc. Although it is still made of wood, it is as hard as steel. To form the proper tool, roll Intelligence + Repair against the fetishes' Gnosis rating.

### Bouncing Rock

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 5
Gnoza: 7
Strona PDF: 54
Strona drukowana: 54

Pełny opis:

This rock, smoothed by years in a river will bounce off of obstacles and hit multiple targets if thrown properly. Roll Dexterity + Athletics versus an eight difficulty. The number of successes is the number of targets the rock will bounce around and hit. Only one “to hit” roll against an 8 difficulty is required, and if it succeeds, all targets are hit. The rock will do the thrower's Str + 1 as damage and will return to the thrower's hand as long as at least one success is scored.

### Prometheus' Torch

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 6
Gnoza: 8
Strona PDF: 54
Strona drukowana: 54

Pełny opis:

This is a torch that forever burns. Its fire never goes out, even when dunked in water. If used as a weapon (Dexterity + Melee), it is difficulty 5 to resist, causing one wound level. It has another property, though. It gives its bearer plus one die to Intelligence and allows a roll of Intelligence + Linguistics to understand any language. This is considered to be a sacred relic to the Glass Walkers and they would do much to find it. It is rumored to be in the possession of a lupus deep in the woods, keeping it from humankind.

Gnoza: nie podano
Strona PDF: 53
Strona drukowana: 53

### Tool Talens

Kategoria: Talen
Poziom/ranga: nie podano
Gnoza: 3
Strona PDF: 53
Strona drukowana: 53

Pełny opis:

These various odds and ends found on the ground in the wilderness (nuts, pebbles, bark, etc.) can be used with the Toolstick fetish (above) and operate as nails, screws, etc.

The user must roll Intelligence + Repair against the talen's Gnosis. Each success means that five nails (screws, etc.) are created. The Tool Talens come in a sack which holds 50 potential tools. Once they are used up, the user must use Rite of Binding to create more potential tools from found objects.
