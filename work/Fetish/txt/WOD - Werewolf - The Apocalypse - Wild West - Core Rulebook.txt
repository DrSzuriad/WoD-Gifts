## FETYSZE

### Medicine Bag

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: varies
Gnoza: varies
Strona PDF: 285
Strona drukowana: 282

Pełny opis:

Medicine Bags are fairly common among the native Garou, and are used for a multitude of purposes. These can include: Peace (Level 1, Gnosis 5), which increases the difficulty of all Rage rolls by +3; Protection from Spirits (Level 3, Gnosis 6), which prevents an uninvited spirit from manifesting within 100 feet of the bearer; Healing (Level 4, Gnosis 8), which allows the bearer to heal at twice the normal rate; and Stealth (Level 2, Gnosis 4), which reduces all Stealth-related difficulties by two.

The most powerful Medicine Bags (Level 5, Gnosis 8) allow a Garou to roam among humans in any form and still appear human (though violent acts are still perceived normally), or fool Wyrm-minions into perceiving the bearer as a kindred spirit. Many Medicine Bags do more than one of the above in conjunction, but are always of appropriately high level and require very high Gnosis to activate. Appropriate spirits must be bound into Medicine Bags, and all but the most powerful abilities of the Medicine Bag require concentration in order to operate properly.

Medicine Bags are always created by the elders of the Uktena and Wendigo tribes, and are seldom awarded to anyone from other tribes. These powerful fetishes are usually attuned to the wearer when they are presented.

### Dream Catcher

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 2
Gnoza: 4
Strona PDF: 285
Strona drukowana: 282

Pełny opis:

The Dream Catcher is a simple hoop of wood, designed with a web of sinew in its center. When activated, this fetish captures and holds any Banes attempting to possess the owner. This fetish is actually fairly common, but few Europeans know how to make one work properly.

To create a Dream Catcher, a Protection, Guardian or Weaver-spirit must be coerced into the sinew web.

### Turtle Necklace

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 3
Gnoza: 7
Strona PDF: 285
Strona drukowana: 282

Pełny opis:

One of the few remaining artifacts of the lost Croatan, this turtle-shell necklace is a potent fetish of protection. When activated, it grants its wearer an extra soak die against all attacks, including silver, for a scene. The few Turtle Necklaces were gifts from the Croatan to the other Pure Ones, and some doubt that any will be left in a century or so. Presumably a Turtle-spirit must be bound into a necklace to create this fetish, but no other tribe has had any luck doing so.

### Klaive

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 4
Gnoza: 6
Strona PDF: 285
Strona drukowana: 282

Pełny opis:

A klaive is a ritual dagger of the European Garou. Klaives are rare weapons, for they are made of silver and their manufacture and use is deadly to the Garou. A klaive is about the size of a Bowie knife, and a Garou who carries one loses one point from his effective Gnosis Rating. A War-spirit is usually bound into a klaive, allowing it to do aggravated damage to non-Garou enemies. Without the spirit, the klaive only causes aggravated damage to other shapechangers; however, a Garou need not attune a klaive without a spirit.

Pulling a klaive on another Garou is considered a grave action, for a klaive duel is almost always to the death.

The difficulty to attack with a klaive is 6, and it inflicts Str +2 damage. Garou can’t soak this damage because it’s a silver weapon,

### Spirit Axe

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 4
Gnoza: 6
Strona PDF: 286
Strona drukowana: 283

Pełny opis:

The Uktena fashion spirit axes to help them battle the minions of the Wyrm. These weapons act as normal axes when used against an enemy, save when their target is possessed by a Bane or spirit. In the latter event, the spirit axe ignores the physical person, simply passing through solid flesh as if it weren’t there, and attacks the spirit instead.

To create a spirit axe, a Predator-spirit must be convinced to join with the specially crafted weapon.

### War Bow

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 4
Gnoza: 7
Strona PDF: 286
Strona drukowana: 283

Pełny opis:

War bows are intricately carved from bone and often adorned with numerous feathers and beads. These powerful weapons are much like the klaives of the European Garou in that they are usually passed down to descendants in the same family. The war bow is a weapon first, and fires arrows at a -2 difficulty, while inflicting Strength +2 aggravated damage.

To create a war bow, a War-spirit, and sometimes an Ancestor-spirit, must be bound within the weapon.

### Spirit Whistle

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 4
Gnoza: 8
Strona PDF: 286
Strona drukowana: 283

Pełny opis:

When activated, this small bone whistle emits a powerful shrieking noise that causes great pain to all spirits within the wielder’s line of sight. Any spirits present must roll Gnosis, difficulty 6, and score equal to or greater than the user’s successes on the activation roll in order to avoid fleeing in pain and fear.

In order to make a Spirit Whistle, a Screech Owl-spirit, or a spirit of Madness, Screaming or Discord must be bound into the whistle.

### Grand Klaive

Kategoria: Fetysz
Poziom/ranga: 5
Gnoza: 7
Strona PDF: 286
Strona drukowana: 283

Pełny opis:

These are the legendary weapons of European Garou heroes. Grand klaives are as big as swords and, like their smaller counterparts, are forged from silver. A Garou who carries a grand klaive loses two from his effective Gnosis Rating. As with klaives, the grand klaives usually have War-spirits bound into them, causing aggravated damage to non-shapechanger enemies. Those few without War-spirits need not be attuned to the wearer.

Grand klaives are extremely rare and are often passed down through family lineages, especially by the Silver Fangs and Shadow Lords. In addition to a War-spirit, some have an additional spirit — such as a Gnosis-spirit to grant magical aid to the user, or even an Ancestor-spirit — bound within them. The second spirits seldom mind being bound within them because these weapons are so honored and legendary.

The difficulty to attack with a klaive is 7, and it inflicts Str + 4 damage. Garou can’t soak this damage because it’s a silver weapon.

## TALENY

### Gaia’s Breath

Kategoria: Talen
Poziom/ranga: nie podano
Gnoza: 5
Strona PDF: 286
Strona drukowana: 283

Pełny opis:

Gaia’s Breath is a small dried gourd marked with symbols of praise to Gaia. When crushed and spilled over an open wound, the powdered gourd heals up to four Health Levels, even if the damage is aggravated.

To create a Gaia’s Breath talen, a spirit of Healing must be bound within the gourd.

### Bane Arrows

Kategoria: Talen
Poziom/ranga: nie podano
Gnoza: 4
Strona PDF: 287
Strona drukowana: 284

Pełny opis:

These obsidian-headed arrows are able to home in on Banes, whether they are visible or not. These arrows hit automatically and inflict three dice of aggravated damage. The bite of a Bane arrow is so painful that no Bane can resist crying out in pain.

To create a Bane arrow, one of the following spirits must be bound into the arrow: War, Air or Pain.

### Vision Pool

Kategoria: Talen
Poziom/ranga: nie podano
Gnoza: 6
Strona PDF: 287
Strona drukowana: 284

Pełny opis:

A vision pool is a simple powder which, when added to a clear pool of water, shows visions of the future. These talens are used for divination, but are often convey vague messages, requiring an Enigmas roll (difficulty 6) to comprehend.

To create a vision pool, a Divination-spirit must be bound into the ashes of burned peyote.
